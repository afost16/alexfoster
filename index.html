about

I'm a UX & CX researcher based in Seattle, currently working as a customer experience strategic research intern at Seattle City Light while finishing my Master's in Human Centered Design & Engineering at the University of Washington.

Outside of research, I'm drawn to the small, human details of how people actually live with the systems designed for them — which is probably why so much of my work ends up being about care, whether that's for a caregiver, a rider, or a teammate stuck in a hard conversation.
